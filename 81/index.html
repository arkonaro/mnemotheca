    Mnemotheca

    The beautiful things that pass before my eyes, and that I don't want to forget.

    Mnemo-, memory; -theca, the place where something is kept, as in bibliotheca and pinacotheca. The name comes from the Mnemosyne atlas that Aby Warburg assembled between 1924 and 1929: panels of black cloth on which photographs from distant ages sat side by side, and from their neighbourhood came meanings that no single image held on its own.

    Here I collect architecture, painting, cinema, photography, design, texts and science. Each plate stands alone, with its number. The connections are made by whoever is looking.
